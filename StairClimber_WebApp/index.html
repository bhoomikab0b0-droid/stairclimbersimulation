      How the autonomy works

      Sense
      • 2D LiDAR: 160 beams in a vertical fan on the left front unit
      • RGB-D depth camera (87° × 58°) on the frame
      • Ultrasonic rangers: two forward, one on each side
      • 4 IR cliff sensors, IMU, wheel and rack encoders, bumper switches
      Perceive & decide
      • LiDAR scan segmentation, then a Kalman filter over 12 scans
      • The depth camera confirms a riser spans the full width, finds obstacles and classifies the surface
      • Gait library LOW / STANDARD / HIGH with safety limits and obstacle rules
      • On the landing: occupancy map, A* path planning, a 180° turn and re-alignment on the walls
      Act
      • PID loops on each rack, the frame width, travel distance and heading
      • IMU pitch PID trims the front rack while the back units lift
      • Moves end on sensor events (bumper, cliff sensor), so wheel slip cannot fool it
      • Mop + vacuum head sweeps each tread; carpet switches to vacuum only

      Runs entirely in your browser on a rigid-body contact model: the body's resting pose comes from its wheel and pad contacts, with collisions, wheel slip and sensor noise. The same perception and control algorithms also run in the project's PyBullet physics version.
